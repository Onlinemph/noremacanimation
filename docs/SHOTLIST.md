# ОБЪЕКТ 9 / OBJECT 9 — shot list

Antarctica, midwinter 1989 (polar night: it is dark 24 hours a day). A small American team from
Amundsen–Scott South Pole Station picks up a Russian distress signal from a place that is on no map.
They drive a Sno-Cat 340 km across the plateau and find a secret Soviet station, Object 9, half-buried
in the ice. The base drilled into a subglacial lake at 3,700 m and brought something up. The staff are
still there. Once the team is inside, the blast door seals. They pair up with the last living Soviet
conscript, arm themselves from the armory and the rear-echelon reserve crates, and fight through the
base to the vehicle hangar, where an old Kharkovchanka snow tractor is their only way out.

Total runtime 146 s at 24 fps, 1280x720, 2.39:1 letterbox (post `bar: 0.12`) unless noted.

## Characters (used on the armory cards and subtitles)

- DR. ELLEN HOLLIS, glaciologist (NSF). Takes the KS-23.
- RAY OKAFOR, station radio operator. SPSh flare pistol and a fire axe.
- ANDERS LUNDQVIST, station mechanic. PPSh-41 from a crate of 1944 reserve stock.
- Ml. SERZHANT MIKHAIL "MISHA" VOLKOV, 19, the last of the garrison, found barricaded in the armory. AKS-74U, RGD-5 grenades.

## Visual language

- Cold side: deep blue-black exteriors, blue-white snow and flashlight light, aurora green/violet.
- Warm/danger side: sodium-orange work lights, red emergency lamps, muzzle-flash orange-white.
- Soviet 1980s industrial: green-grey painted steel, cream upper walls, concrete, stencilled Cyrillic, red star, posters.
- Horror is mostly what you almost see: silhouettes, flicker, fog, backlight. Gore is wet, dark, and specific when shown.
- Everything gets film grain, slight aberration, letterbox.
